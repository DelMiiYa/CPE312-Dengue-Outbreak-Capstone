# Brief — T04 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) figures and findings
Status: TODO · Written by: orchestrator · Date: 2026-11-09

## Goal
Generate publication-ready EDA figures and a quantitative summary from `data/processed/panel.csv` to support poster section **R3** (and provide visual evidence for **R1** and **R2**).

## Why
Serves requirement(s): R3 (EDA findings, visualization). Depends on: T03 (PASS). Can run in parallel with T05.

## Inputs (exact paths)
- `data/processed/panel.csv`
- `config/params.yaml`

## Outputs (exact paths)
- `src/eda/run_eda.py` — reproducible script generating all figures and stats
- `results/figures/eda_seasonality.png` — monthly seasonal profile of dengue cases alongside rainfall (`precip_sum_mm`) and temperature (`temp_mean_c`)
- `results/figures/eda_lag_correlation.png` — lag correlation (lags 0 to 4 months) between weather variables (`precip_sum_mm`, `temp_mean_c`, `humidity_mean_pct`) and monthly dengue cases, **computed on train years (1993–2015) only**
- `results/figures/eda_province_heterogeneity.png` — comparison of case distributions across provinces demonstrating strong baseline differences across provinces (motivating the per-province threshold `mean_p + 1*SD_p` rather than a single national threshold)
- `results/figures/eda_annual_trend_covid.png` — annual total cases and zero-case share (1993–2022) showing epidemic peaks (e.g., 1998, 2013, 2015), train/val/test boundaries, and the 2020–2022 COVID-era drop
- `results/eda_stats.json` — all numerical statistics cited in `eda_summary.md` (peak month, lag correlations, top/bottom province means, annual totals) generated by code
- `results/eda_summary.md` — concise interpretation of each figure (every number copied from `results/eda_stats.json`) and explicit recommendation of the **1 primary key chart** for Poster Box R3

## Constraints
- Follow `_agent/AGENTS.md` and `_agent/docs/SSOT.md`.
- **Time-series / anti-leakage rule:** Any correlation or threshold-motivating statistic (`eda_lag_correlation.png` and `eda_province_heterogeneity.png`) must be computed on **train years (1993–2015) only** (`config/params.yaml -> split.train_years`). Only the descriptive timeline (`eda_annual_trend_covid.png`) should span 1993–2022, with vertical dashed lines marking the Train (1993–2015), Validation (2016–2017), and Test (2018–2022) splits.
- Save figures at `dpi=300` with clear titles, axis labels with units, and readable font sizes suitable for an A2 poster.
- Do not invent numbers in `results/eda_summary.md`; every number must come from `results/eda_stats.json`.

## Out of scope
Feature matrix creation, target engineering, or model training (covered in T05–T07).

## Acceptance criteria (run these, paste real output in the handoff)
1. `python src/eda/run_eda.py` runs to completion with exit code 0.
2. All 4 PNG files exist in `results/figures/` and are non-empty (> 20 KB each):
   `python -c "from pathlib import Path; figs=['eda_seasonality.png','eda_lag_correlation.png','eda_province_heterogeneity.png','eda_annual_trend_covid.png']; print({f: (Path('results/figures')/f).stat().st_size for f in figs})"`
3. `results/eda_stats.json` is valid JSON and contains the exact numbers cited in `results/eda_summary.md`.
4. Add unit test file `tests/test_eda.py` verifying that `results/eda_stats.json` and the 4 figures exist and that lag correlations in `eda_stats.json` are computed strictly on train years `1993–2015`. Run `pytest` and paste output.

## Known pitfalls
- Computing lag correlations across provinces: compute within-province lags first (`groupby('province_en').shift(k)`) so lags never bleed across province boundaries, and consider both pooled correlation (on province-standardized or log1p cases) and median within-province correlation across the 77 provinces (since raw case counts vary by orders of magnitude across provinces). Report both in `eda_stats.json`!
